I am an Assistant Professor of Electrical Engineering at the University of Kashmir, where I previously served as the youngest-ever Department Chair from 2022 to 2025. Before joining academia, I worked as a field engineer at Kashmir's power utility. I hold a B.Tech. in Electrical Engineering (2013) and an M.Tech. in Power Systems (2015) from NIT Srinagar.

My research focuses on the intersection of power system dynamics and machine learning. I am currently developing a zero-allocation fused 6D robust dynamic synchrophasor state estimation framework for low-inertia power systems and researching physics-informed manifold embeddings for real-time grid monitoring.

Beyond grid stability, I lead the development of **KashmirBuild**, an open-source building energy efficiency platform tailored to Kashmir's cold climate. I am also currently pursuing an MSc in Financial Engineering at WorldQuant University to expand my quantitative time-series modeling capabilities.
